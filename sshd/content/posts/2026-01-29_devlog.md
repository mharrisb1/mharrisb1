# Google Sheets Client Refactor

It's been 2 weeks of nights and weekends when I could fit it in but I'm finally at the tail end of the refactor for the DuckDB Google Sheet extension where I'm swapping the tightly coupled logic for auth, transport, and API into a new, clean architecure design. I tested everything and we have 100% feature parity (for what was tested) and no noticeable performance regressions.

Here is a summary from Claude:

